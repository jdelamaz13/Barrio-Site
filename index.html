What we do

Building the data layer for public safety

Barrio Data Strategies designs mobile and web software that helps people understand what's happening around them and act on it — combining real-time reporting, mapping, and analytics.

Real-time reporting

Geo-referenced, community-sourced incident data captured the moment it matters.

Actionable analytics

Trends, hotspots, and insight for individuals, communities, and public-sector partners.

Privacy by design

Sensitive data is protected with strict access controls and encryption in transit.
